# BetterNote v1.0.0

Hey everyone! 👋 

BetterNote is a little hobby project I’ve been vibe coding because I really wanted a simple, lightweight handwritten note-taking app on Windows without all the bloat, sign-ins, and subscriptions of mainstream tools. 

Just a clean piece of paper, smooth pens, and good vibes.

---

### The Story & Vibe
I built this primarily for myself as a passion project to have a calm space to scribble, sketch ideas, and take quick notes during the day. It's completely free, runs 100% offline on your machine, and saves your notes into simple, human-readable JSON files—so your notes are always yours.

---

### What's in this first build (v1.0.0)

- **Smooth Inking**: Simple, pressure-sensitive vector strokes with Bézier curve smoothing that feels natural whether you're using a drawing tablet, stylus, or mouse.
- **The Essentials**:
  - **Pen**: For writing and sketching.
  - **Highlighter**: Blends nicely over existing strokes so it doesn't cover your writing.
  - **Eraser**: Quickly removes strokes you don't need.
  - **Selection Tool**: Circle strokes and drag them around to reorganize your thoughts.
  - **Thickness Slider**: A neat floating vertical slider to dial in stroke size quickly.
- **Colors That Match**:
  - Quick-click color swap button with a full color picker.
  - Pinned swatches bar (right-click any color to unpin it).
  - A relaxed, soothing palette inspired by the app icon.
- **Multi-page Notebooks**: Add, delete, and flip through pages easily.
- **Zoom & Free Pan**: Zoom in up to 400% for fine details, or hold `Space` and drag to pan around.
- **Exporting**: Export your pages to PDF or images (`.png`, `.jpg`) whenever you need to share them.
- **Distraction-Free Mode**: Hit `F11` to go fullscreen with just your canvas and the floating toolbar.
- **Standalone Windows `.exe`**: Bundled as a single `BetterNote.exe`—no need to install Python or configure anything, just double-click and write.
